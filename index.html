🎮 How to Read Your First Book (What You're Doing Wrong)

Your problem: You're trying Prophecies of Glory (mystery 12). Stop. It requires 3 Sky + 2 Lantern, and you don't have the foundation. Start with these instead:

Read FIRST (mystery 4-5): Perugian Diaries, What Does Not Bark, The Locksmith's Dream vol.4. All found in Dispensary after unlocking it.
Step 1: Slot a Book into a Reading Desk. The book shows you what it requires (e.g., "2 Sky").
Step 2: Add a Soul Card (character). This gives 1-4 Principles. Any Soul works—they're not consumed.
Step 3: Add a Skill (optional) If you have one matching the book's Principles, slot it. If not, proceed anyway.
Step 4: Add Other Boosters Memory (2-6 Principles), Tool (1-3), Beverage, Weather. Pick one of each type.
Step 5: Click Read If your total ≥ book's requirement, you win a Lesson + Memory. If not, you lose the items and the book corrupts.

📋 The 3 Game Phases

PHASE 1: BOOTSTRAP (Day 1-3)

Goal: Get 3 guaranteed skills, unlock 6-8 easy rooms, build initial Memories.

  • Unlock Motley Tower → Dispensary. Catalog books there to get 3 guaranteed books (Perugian Diaries, What Does Not Bark, Locksmith's Dream).
  • Read these 3 books to get Strings & Songs, Edicts Inviolable, Watchman's Paradoxes.
  • Unlock Kitchen and Well next. These have unlimited free drinks and food.
  • Unlock Reading Room—it has the most shelf space for books you'll master.
  • DO NOT reply to Rhonwen Trust letter yet—it triggers book corruption. You can ignore it for 10+ days.
  • Talk to your free assistant about topics to boost their Principles for rooms (costs nothing).
  • Don't craft yet. Focus on reading easy books and unlocking rooms.
PHASE 2: EXPANSION (Day 4-15)

Goal: Read 15-20 books to collect the 22 guaranteed Memories, unlock Curia/Solar Temple, start crafting.

  • By now you should have 3-5 Skills. Level them to lv2-3 by reading books and re-reading mastered ones.
  • Unlock rooms in Baronial area. You need both your free assistant (don't hire yet) and crafted boosters.
  • Start crafting basic items: Amethyst Ampoule (Knock), Dearday Lens (Lantern), Leathy (Moth/Nectar).
  • Hire Unusual Help at tavern (costs money) once you have 2-3 ways to boost a Principle to 14+.
  • Reach Solar Temple and Curia. These open up late-game rooms.
  • Gather 22 guaranteed Memories from reading different books. Use these for future skill leveling.
PHASE 3: MASTERY (Day 15+)

Goal: Craft persistent Memories, level key Skills to lv5+, unlock 80% of rooms, prep for ending.

  • Craft persistent Memories: Wormwood Dream (Moon/Winter), Wind-in-Waiting (Sky), Curious Hunch (Knock).
  • Unlock Caverns, Gaol, Sisterhood—highest-level rooms. These need crafted boosters + Unusual Help.
  • Find Numen books (hidden throughout house). Each gives a 5-Principle Memory—game-changer.
  • Level Edicts Martial, Strings & Songs, Sights & Sensations to lv5-7. These are your crafting backbone.
  • Craft Encaustum Terminale Inks (Uzult, Marakat, Nillycant, Porphyrine). These unlock 18+ mystery books.
  • Prep for ending: gather Numen + Ink + Journal with 25 of one Principle = write a History = beat the game.

⚙️ Key Mechanics You're Missing

Soul Cards (Your Fragments)

You start with 2. Unlock more by binding Skills to the Wisdom Tree (the card grid in lower right). Each slot gives you a new Soul card type when filled. You get 9 total. Each Soul can be used multiple times per day—they're not consumed. But they get fatigued after use and must rest overnight.

Lessons vs. Memories

Lessons (finite, yellow cards) come from reading books. They unlock new Skills or level existing ones. Use them strategically—don't waste them. Memories (common, blue cards) come from considering objects, reading mastered books, or crafting. They're temporary—they fade at day end. Reusable is fine.

Upgrading Assistants for Room Unlocking

When you talk to an assistant (free or hired), you can give them ONE of each: Memory, Soul card, Tool, Beverage, Food. This boosts their Principles for that day. Some assistants (blacksmith, engineer, poet) have a 6th slot for special items (metal, fuel, flowers). You only unlock one room per assistant per day.

Don't Upgrade Soul Cards Yet

Soul evolution (turning a Soul into +Soul or ++Soul for more Principles) sounds good but costs 3 actions per day permanently. Early game, skip it. Even late game, only upgrade if you have spare actions.

Weather is Free

Every day you get random weather (Sunny, Fog, Snow, etc.). Each adds 2-3 of a Principle. Use it! It's renewable and free.

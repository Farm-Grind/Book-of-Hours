📚 Book of Hours: Complete Wiki

⚙️ Core Mechanics: 6 Systems to Master

Book of Hours has 6 interconnected systems. Understanding these is the key to beating the game.

1. SOUL ELEMENTS (Your Power)

What are they?

Soul Elements are colored cards with aspects. You start with 2 (usually Health + one other). Each:

  • Has aspects: e.g., Phost = 2 Sky + 1 Lantern
  • Gets used: Slot into desks to read books, into rooms to unlock, into workstations to craft
  • Gets fatigued: After use, needs to rest (~1 day) before reusing
  • Can be maladied: From contaminated books or failed reads. Cure at beds with matching drinks
  • Can evolve: Combine 2 identical + skill (attuned to that element) at special workstation = +Element (more power)

The 9 Soul Types:

Health: Heart/Nectar/Scale • Chor: Grail/Heart/Lantern • Ereb: Edge/Grail/Moon • Fet: Rose/Grail/Knock • Mettle: Edge/Forge/Knock • Phost: Sky/Lantern/Forge • Shapt: Knock/Winter/Forge • Trist: Moon/Winter/Rose • Wist: Scale/Winter/Moon

2. SKILLS (Your Spellbook)

What are they?

Skills are power cards with 2 aspects each. They:

  • Read books: Slot into desk + soul + book to solve mysteries
  • Craft items: Slot into workstations to make inks, memories, tools
  • Level up: Spend 1 Lesson + matching memories to increase aspects (Lesson becomes permanent skill)
  • Get attuned: Commit to Tree of Wisdom to get Soul element + unlock evolution stations

Getting Skills:

  • From Lessons: Read any book first time → get unique Lesson → use Consider to turn into Skill
  • 3 guaranteed early: Catalog Dispensary for Strings & Songs, Edicts Inviolable, Watchman's Paradoxes
  • Total in game: 63+ skills. Get them by reading books

3. READING BOOKS (The Core Challenge)

The Mystery System:

Every book has a Mystery (2–25 aspects) and Principle (aspect type like Sky or Knock). To read:

  1. Catalog it: Consider book with any soul → reveals its Mystery level
  2. Take to matching desk: Sky Mystery? Use Sky desk
  3. Slot: Soul + Skill + Book + (optionally) Memory/Tool/Weather
  4. Check if you hit the Mystery: Total aspects ≥ Mystery? You succeed
    Less? Get 3 random chances during reading to boost (risky)
  5. Reward: First read = Lesson (becomes Skill) + Memory. Re-reads = same Memory (renewable)

Contamination & Curses:

Some books have curses or contaminations that cause maladies if you use certain souls:

  • Avoid: Use a soul element that doesn't match the curse's type
  • Cure: At desk, use book + skill + enough aspect (e.g., "cure needs 7 Knock")
  • Ignore: Some curses don't affect common souls (e.g., Chionic doesn't malady Wist)

4. CRAFTING (Making Powerful Items)

The Recipe System:

Workstations have recipes at 3 levels: Prentice (5 aspect), Scholar (10), Keeper (15). To craft:

  1. Find the right workstation (e.g., Kitchen Range for food)
  2. Choose a Skill (must have the recipe)—determines output
  3. Slot: Soul + Skill + Inputs (varies: Memories, ingredients, tools, inks)
  4. Hit the aspect cost (e.g., 10 Forge for Gervinite)
  5. Get the item back (permanent until used, or consumable)

5. ROOM UNLOCKING (Exploration)

How It Works:

  1. Room shows requirement: "6 Heart OR 6 Sky" (you only need ONE)
  2. Get an assistant (free helper each game, or hire for ~50 pence)
  3. Boost their aspects via Talk: give Memory + Soul + Tool + Drink + Food
  4. Once they have enough, send to room (costs 1 day)
  5. Room unlocks permanently

Pro Tips:

  • Only need one aspect: Ignore the others. 6 Heart alone is fine
  • Assistant aspects stack: Memory (2–6) + Soul (1–4) + Tool (1) + Drink (1–2) + Food (1–2) = 7–17 total
  • Money is free: Earn 6 pence per action at tavern. Hire costs 50–100. No scarcity
  • One room per assistant per day: Can't unlock 2 rooms same day with same helper

6. TREE OF WISDOM (Soul Evolution Hub)

What It Does:

9-part wheel where you slot Skills. Each gives you a new Soul element + "attunes" skill for evolution.

  • Each Wisdom: Has 2 possible Soul rewards (e.g., Horomachistry gives Fet OR Phost)
  • Preview before: Hover over Wisdom to see which Soul you'll get
  • Limited uses: 9 Wisdom slots → max 9 new Souls (can evolve existing ones too)
  • Strategy: Use early on Wisdoms for key soul types. Defer others to late-game

🎮 The Route: Progression Guide

Optimized path through Book of Hours in 3 phases. No time limits—you control pacing.

PHASE 1: BOOTSTRAP (Days 1–3)

Goal: 3 guaranteed skills, 6–8 early rooms, renewable resources.

  • Day 1 – Morning: Read Journal twice at any desk (Lantern desk preferred). First read = any memory, second read = match aspect.
  • Day 1 – Midday: Go to Motley Tower: Dispensary (6 Heart OR 6 Moth). Catalog all books. Get 3 guaranteed: Perugian, Bark, Locksmith.
  • Day 1 – Afternoon: Read all 3 books at Eva Desk. Each gives Lesson → Skill: Strings & Songs, Edicts Inviolable, Watchman's Paradoxes.
  • Day 1 – Evening: Unlock Kitchen (7 Nectar OR 7 Forge) + Well (7 Nectar).
  • Day 2: Unlock Reading Room (8 Lantern OR 8 Sky).
  • Key Resources: Weather is free daily (2–3 aspects). Isle-Water unlimited. Re-read guaranteed books for free Memories daily.
PHASE 2: EXPANSION (Days 4–15)

Goal: Curia/Solar Temple, first crafting chains, 22 guaranteed Memories.

  • Days 4–6: Level core skills to lv3–4. Unlock Grand Ascent (10 various).
  • Days 7–9: Unlock Solar Temple (12 Sky OR 12 Lantern) + Alchemist's Glassware (10 Forge OR 10 Winter).
  • Days 10–12: Start crafting: Amethyst Ampoule (5 Knock), basic Forge items.
  • Days 13–15: Hire first assistant. Unlock Gaol entrance or first Curia room.
PHASE 3: MASTERY (Days 16+)

Goal: Persistent Memories, unlock 80% of house, endgame (History writing).

  • Persistent Memories (critical): Wormwood Dream (hardest recipe), Wind-in-Waiting (easiest), Curious Hunch
  • Ink Chains: Uzult, Marakat, Nillycant, Porphyrine, Orpiment—each needs 15-aspect recipe + ingredient
  • Unlock Gaol/Caverns: Highest rooms (15–17 aspects). One per day max.
  • Find Numen Books: Hidden in vaults. Give 5-Principle Memories (game-changer).
  • Writing History: Journal requires Numen (5 aspect) + Inks (3 different) + 25 of one Principle total.
⚠️ Early Game Notes:
• Don't reply to Rhonwen letter early (keeps books safe from contamination)
• Weather is FREE every day. Use it!
• Tools are FREE and reusable. Hoard in one room
• One room per assistant per day (even with multiple assistants)
• You can only have one of each Memory at once (disappears at dawn if not used)

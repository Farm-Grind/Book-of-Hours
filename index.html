📖 How to Read Your First Book

Step 1: Find a Reading DeskYou start with a desk in the Librarian's Quarters. This is where you read books.
Step 2: Slot a BookDrag a book into the desk. The book will show you what it requires (usually 2 Principles for early books).
Step 3: Boost with a SkillEvery book teaches a Skill. Slot a Skill card that matches the book's Principles. If you don't have one, that's okay—you'll just need other boosts.
Step 4: Add a SoulSlot a Soul card (character). This gives 1-4 Principles. Try any Soul—they're not used up.
Step 5: Click ReadIf your total Principles ≥ the book's requirement, you get the book's Skill Lesson + Memory.
If You FailYou lose the slotted items. The book becomes corrupted. Start fresh with easier books (mystery level 2-6).

🎯 Early Game Strategy

Don't reply to the Rhonwen Trust letter yet—it triggers book corruption. You can ignore it early.
Unlock these rooms first: Motley Tower (Dispensary—3 guaranteed books!), Kitchen, Well.
Read easy books first. Check a book's "mystery level"—read 2-6 first, save 12+ for later.
Collect the 22 guaranteed Memories from different book types. Use those to read harder books.
Don't craft yet. Early game is about exploring and reading books. Crafting matters mid/late game.
Your current problem: You're reading mystery 12+ books without the foundation. Read "Perugian Diaries" or "What Does Not Bark" first (mystery 4-5). Much easier.
